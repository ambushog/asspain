COVID-19 Relief

At the height of the pandemic, Amrik Singh directed his business income into helping others.

In Spain

For a full year he put his business earnings into relief, working with local councils to deliver food and essentials to stranded immigrants and Spanish residents who had lost their income.

In India

During the brutal second wave he worked with the Delhi Sikh Gurdwara Management Committee and young Punjabis to supply oxygen cylinders to hospitals, and gave direct support to Granthi and Pathi Singh families in Punjab.
