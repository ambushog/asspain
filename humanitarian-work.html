Humanitarian Work

Wherever people suffer — war zones, floods, pandemics — Amrik Singh shows up. These are the main missions.

Missions

  • Ukraine War Relief — over a year near the front line.
  • Valencia DANA Floods — eight months of rescue and rebuilding.
  • Anti-Trafficking — helping women escape exploiters.
  • COVID-19 Relief — a year of aid in Spain and oxygen for India.
  • Farmers' Protest Support — solidarity from abroad.
  • Immigrant Support & Repatriation — daily help for new arrivals and grieving families.
