Amrik Singh

Amrik Singh — widely known online as Amrik Singh Spain — is a Sikh community leader and humanitarian based in Valencia, Spain. He is not a politician: his work is Seva, selfless service to anyone in need.

Who is Amrik Singh of Spain?

Amrik Singh grew up in a Punjabi family devoted to public service and Sikh values, and now lives and works in Spain. He is followed by more than 227,000 people across TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, where he documents life, service and Sikh culture.

What does Amrik Singh do?

He leads and joins relief missions, stands with newly arrived immigrants, helps families bring home loved ones who die abroad, and works against fraud and human trafficking. He says he does not accept donations for personal gain.
