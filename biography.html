Biography of Amrik Singh Spain

No tree reaches the sky unless its roots are deeply nourished. The service, courage and humanity of Amrik Singh Spain are an inheritance from his parents.

Roots: a family of service

Father — Jathedar Dr. Darshan Singh Sidhu: a respected scholar, writer and official at Guru Nanak Dev University, Amritsar. During the Punjabi Suba movement he led young people on the front line, and was given the title “Jathedar” for his leadership and sacrifice.

Mother — Surjit Kaur: a woman who stood against injustice and faced imprisonment in defence of human rights. She devoted her life to keeping people connected to Gurbani, supporting Granthi and Pathi Singhs, and funding weddings for girls from poor families.

In this home Amrik Singh learned never to bow to tyranny and always to reach out to people in need.

A life of service across crises

His work includes over a year helping civilians in Ukraine, eight months of recovery after the Valencia DANA floods, rescuing women from trafficking, support for India's farmers' protest and a full year of COVID-19 relief.

Every day he also helps new immigrants and families repatriating loved ones to Punjab.

Refusing personal gain

He says he has never needed personal wealth and does not accept money for his work. He has been honoured in Spain and at the European Parliament in Brussels.

Mission

“Sewa is not politics or a profession; it is the harvest of the values inherited from my parents and the philosophy of our Gurus. As long as there is breath in this body, any human being suffering anywhere in the world is our own flesh and blood.” — Amrik Singh Spain
