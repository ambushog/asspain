Valencia DANA Floods Relief

After the catastrophic DANA floods in Valencia, Amrik Singh led eight months of continuous relief and reconstruction.

Rescue and relief

Coordinated with Gurdwara committees and the Sangat to deliver food, drinking water and medicine door to door.

Led more than two hundred volunteers into the worst-hit streets.

Clean-up and rebuilding

Cleared mud, debris and dead animals from homes, streets and underground car parks.

Rebuilt and restored homes of low-income families left without help from insurers or authorities.

FAQ

How long did Amrik Singh help after the DANA floods?

About eight months of continuous work in the Valencia region.
