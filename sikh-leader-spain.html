Sikh Leader in Spain

A Sikh leader is a servant first. In Spain, Amrik Singh leads by example — organising volunteers, standing with immigrants and serving people of every faith and background.

Leadership through service

When the DANA floods hit Valencia, Amrik Singh worked with Gurdwara committees and the Sangat, building volunteer teams that delivered food, water and medicine and cleared mud from homes and garages. See the full story.

Guiding the Sikh community in Spain

He helps newcomers with paperwork, language barriers and fraudulent agents, and arranges dignified last rites and repatriation for those who die far from home. Read about this daily work.

Open to everyone

Seva has no religion. During COVID-19 he delivered food to stranded immigrants and local Spanish families alike, working with town councils.

FAQ

Who is a Sikh leader in Spain?

Amrik Singh of Valencia is one of the best-known Sikh community leaders and humanitarians in Spain.

Does Amrik Singh charge for help?

No. Community help is offered free of charge.
