Sikh: Faith, Seva & Service

Sikhism is a faith founded in Punjab by Guru Nanak. It teaches one God, equality, honest living and selfless service to all.

Seva — selfless service

Seva means serving others without expecting reward. For Sikhs, labour offered to others is a privilege, not a transaction. This idea shapes everything Amrik Singh does.

Langar — the community kitchen

Every Gurdwara serves free vegetarian meals to all people, regardless of religion, caste, gender or status.

Sangat — community

The Sangat is the congregation: people who learn, sing and serve together.

FAQ

What is Seva?

Seva is selfless service to others, a central Sikh practice.

What is Langar?

Langar is the free communal meal served in Sikh Gurdwaras to everyone.
