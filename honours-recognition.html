Honours & Recognition

Amrik Singh has been recognised in Spain and Europe, yet says he accepts no money for his work.

Spain

Honoured by the Government of Spain and the Spanish National Police for service during COVID-19 and natural disasters.

European Parliament, Brussels

Invited to the European Parliament in Brussels and honoured for relief work in Ukraine and across Europe — a proud moment for the Punjabi diaspora.

Without personal gain

He does not accept donations or payment; his work is funded from his own business.
