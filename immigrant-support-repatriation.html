Immigrant Support & Repatriation

Beyond big disasters, helping people is Amrik Singh's daily work.

Support for new immigrants

Help with language barriers, bureaucracy and translation, and protection against fraudulent agents.

Repatriation of the deceased

When workers die abroad or bodies are unidentified, he helps with identification, contacts families and sends remains home to Punjab. He also arranges full last rites with dignity.

FAQ

Does Amrik Singh help immigrants for free?

Yes — community help is offered without charge.
