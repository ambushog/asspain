Ukraine War Relief

When the war turned cities into danger zones, Amrik Singh left safety behind and spent more than a year in affected areas.

What he did

Delivered food rations and essentials directly to trapped civilians.

Worked with local Ukrainian volunteers to help evacuate thousands of stranded students and families.

Continued despite several companions being seriously injured during missions.
