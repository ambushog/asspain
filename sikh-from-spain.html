Sikh from Spain

Sikhs have built a growing, welcoming community in Spain. Amrik Singh, a Sikh from Spain, is one of the voices connecting that community with its neighbours.

Sikhs in Valencia and across Spain

The Sikh community gathers around the Gurdwara and the Sangat (congregation), sharing Langar — a free meal for all. In emergencies, these networks mobilise quickly: during the DANA floods Gurdwara committees and volunteers worked side by side.

Sikh values in daily life

Honest work, sharing with others and remembering God are the core of Sikh life. Learn more on the Sikh & Seva page.
